CORE TERMS

Scan:
One stored decision for one instrument at one time.
Episode:
Related suspicious scans grouped into one alert record.
Opening:
The first scan that starts an episode.
Update:
New material evidence added to an open episode.
Escalation:
An update that raises recorded severity.
Closure:
An episode ends after enough valid, quiet trading minutes.
Route A:
Eligible exceptional flow or repricing evidence at the displayed threshold.
Route B:
Moderate same-side flow plus CUSUM persistence and pricing or a fresh OI release.
Flow score:
How unusual current call or put flow is versus its reference behavior.
Pricing score:
How unusual the current options repricing is.
OI confirmation:
Fresh daily open-interest evidence that can support Route B; an OI score alone is not confirmation.
CUSUM persistence:
Accumulated same-side evidence across valid scans.
Severity:
Alert anomaly magnitude, not a probability.
Forecast variance:
Predicted amount of future price variation for the stated horizon.
QLIKE:
A forecast loss measure; lower is better.
Coverage:
Whether needed observations were complete and eligible.
Synthetic mode:
Artificial input used to test software behavior, not market predictive value.

INTERPRET SCORES

Robust anomaly units measure distance from historical behavior. They are not probabilities or automatically Gaussian standard deviations. No score proves insider trading.

WHERE TO LOOK

Overview shows the latest finding and changes. Alerts groups episodes; Scans shows current route evidence beside original opening reasons. Watchlist shows each instrument now. Forecasts shows separate future-variance estimates and later outcomes. Experiments and Scenarios test synthetic behavior. System shows worker, source, data and delivery state. Methods records rule semantics.

DISPLAY KEY

Green = normal stored information or PASS. Amber = threshold crossing, stale data or a warning. Red = actual FAIL or critical state. NONE means no matching record; UNAVAILABLE means the value was not stored here. Raw records and full identifiers are in expandable details and exports.
