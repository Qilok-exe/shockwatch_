HOW TO READ SHOCKWATCH

Read the status and latest findings first. Then check the first-run source audit, per-instrument coverage, quality exclusions, and stock forecasts. The archived synthetic pages document software tests only.

Paper mode
Observes Yahoo market data without orders or notifications.
Stock forecast
EWMA variance of the next observed regular-session five-minute adjusted return. This is separate from alerts.
Flow / pricing score
Unavailable until historical real option observations support independent calibration.
OI confirmation
Requires at least two valid daily snapshots of the same contract; Yahoo release time is uncertain.
Decision interval
A completed regular-session hour plus processing delay. Missing bars exclude the decision.
Stale last trade
Last recorded option trade exceeded the freshness limit; Yahoo quote time itself is uncertain.
Synthetic archive
Earlier artificial scenarios; no evidence of market prediction.

Scores, if introduced after real-data calibration, measure anomalies, not insider-trading probabilities. No current paper score or alert is shown.
